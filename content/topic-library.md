# Topic Library: Viral Educational Shorts

## What makes the two reference Shorts work (and how we differ)

| Reference | Why it spreads | What we take | What we do differently |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arteries + foods (BeHealthy, ~70M views, 30 s) | Cute 3D organ character; a physical metaphor (tiny construction crew scraping plaque); one food per beat with a 2-line label; no voice, so it works in any language | Character + metaphor + labelled list | Our own recurring characters (e.g. "Liv the Liver", "Gus the Gut"), different metaphors (cleaning crews, delivery drivers, firefighters, gardeners), and a 1-second hook question at the start |
| Perfectionism vs Consistency (The Mind Power, ~12M views, 15 s) | A-vs-B race shown as a simple visual; "POV:" caption bar; payoff glow at the end; loops cleanly | A-vs-B structure, POV bar, loop-friendly ending | Different visual languages (plants growing, paths, batteries, bridges), different pairs, and our own colour identity |

**Shared formula**: one idea, one visual metaphor, 15–35 seconds, readable with the sound off, last frame flows back into the first.

## Publishing pattern
- Post 1 to 2 per day per series; keep the same character, colours and title format within a series so viewers recognise it.
- Title formula: `<Number or POV> <Benefit> – <Metaphor>` (e.g. "5 Foods for Your Liver – Meet the Night Cleaners").
- Hook in the first second: a question or a visible problem (clogged pipe, dim battery).
- Loop: the last frame should match or lead into the first frame.
- Hashtags: 3–5 specific tags (#LiverHealth #GutHealth), not dozens of generic ones.
